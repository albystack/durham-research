SCIENTIFIC TARGET
Two conditionally independent, CFTP-certified dimer configurations are sampled
in each frozen edge environment. The primary estimand is the across-environment
covariance of the paired centre heights and spatial height increments. Gamma
(shape=1, scale=1) is the disorder model; all-one weights are the negative
control. The later analysis compares ordinary-log and log-squared descriptions
without constraining the quadratic coefficient to be positive.

SEED AND CENSORING CONTRACT
All 23,800 environment seeds are unique. All 47,600 CFTP seeds are unique and
the two sample seeds within every environment differ. Environments are written
before sampling and checked against deterministic regeneration and SHA-256.
A pair.json scientific row is written only after both streams certify. A capped
stream is retained as censored and must be replayed with its identical weights,
seed, and random-map history; no environment may be discarded or replaced.
